Privacy Policy for ClipRazor

ClipRazor: Lossless Video Cutter does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell personal information.

How the app works

The app operates locally on the user's device. It accesses video files, project files, images, and removable storage only when the user chooses to open, save, import, or export files as part of the app's functionality.

Data collection

ClipRazor does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, user accounts, contact lists, location services, or cloud synchronization.

Data sharing

No user files or personal information are uploaded to the developer or to third-party servers by the app.

Changes

If the app's privacy behavior changes in a future release, this policy will be updated accordingly.
